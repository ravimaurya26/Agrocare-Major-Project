# 🌾 KisaanAgrocare

**KisaanAgrocare** is a smart **digital farming assistant** designed to empower farmers with real-time insights, expert support, and AI-powered tools to make farming more efficient and stress-free.

---

## 🚀 Features

✅ **Smart Chatbot** — Get instant answers to agricultural questions.  
✅ **Crop Diagnosis (AI-based)** — Upload crop images and get health reports.  
✅ **Weather & Market Updates** — Stay informed with real-time data.  
✅ **Expert Connect** — Chat or connect with nearby Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) and agricultural agencies.  
✅ **Community Platform** — Share experiences, tips, and solutions with other farmers.
